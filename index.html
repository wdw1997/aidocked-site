This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have regarding your information.

1. Information We Collect

Account information. When you create an account, we collect your name, business name, email address, and password (stored in encrypted form).

Business and customer data. To provide quoting and scheduling, we collect information you input about your own customers, including names and phone numbers, job details, and any pricing or notes you add.

SMS communications. We collect the content and metadata of text messages sent and received through the Service, including messages generated by our AI on your behalf and customer replies.

Google Calendar data. If you connect Google Calendar, we access your calendar events and free/busy availability solely to schedule, update, check conflicts for, and cancel appointments on your behalf.

Usage information. We may collect information about how you access and use the Service, such as login timestamps, device/browser type, and IP address.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information described above to provide, operate, and maintain the Service, including AI-drafted SMS replies and quotes sent on your behalf; job scheduling and calendar syncing to avoid double-booking; monitoring and improving performance, security, and reliability; managing your subscription and usage-based billing; communicating with you about your account; and complying with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information or your customers' personal information to third parties.

3. How We Share Your Information

We share information only with service providers that help us operate the Service, under contractual obligations restricting them from using it for their own purposes: SignalWire (to send and receive SMS on your behalf), Supabase (to securely host application data), AI/language model providers (to generate SMS reply drafts and quotes), and Creem (to process payments).

Any data originating from Google Workspace APIs (including Google Calendar event details and free/busy availability data) that is shared with our AI/language model providers is used solely to perform the specific function you requested (such as checking for scheduling conflicts) and is never used by us, or by these providers, to train, retrain, or improve any machine learning or artificial intelligence model, whether foundational or otherwise.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text-messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

4. Data Retention

We retain your account and customer data for as long as your account remains active. If you close your account, we will delete or anonymize your personal information within a reasonable period, except where retention is required by law or to resolve disputes.

5. Your Rights and Choices

You may contact us at any time to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, or your customers' personal information you've entered into the Service. You may also opt out of SMS at any time by replying "STOP" to any text message.

6. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/TLS), encrypted password storage, and access controls limiting who can view stored data.

7. International Data Transfer

AI Docked is operated from [your operating location]; however, our service providers (including SignalWire and our hosting infrastructure) may process and store data in the United States or other countries. By using the Service, you understand your information may be transferred to and processed in a country other than your own.

8. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to individuals under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify you directly.
